Colleagues

    

    
    
Offline

    

    

    
    

    

    
Both phones need Sliceview open on screen and online (switching to another app can take you offline for a moment; it reconnects when you come back). The case goes straight from one phone to the other, anonymised, and is not stored anywhere: it closes when the conversation ends.

Send the scans, anonymised

    

    
    

    
DICOM files that open in Sliceview, RadiAnt or any viewer, with every slice. The name, ID, birth date, accession number, doctors, hospital and private tags are removed and the UIDs replaced. Screenshots and dose reports are left out, since their pictures can show the name.

  





  

    
Thick slab

    
Mode

    

    
Thickness

    

    
MIP: the brightest along the slab (vessels, nodules). MinIP: the darkest (airways, emphysema). Average: a smooth thick slice.

  





  

    
Subtraction

    

    

    
    
Each slice is matched with the slice at the same position in the body. If the patient moved between the two scans, edges show as bright and dark lines.
